# 💼 Pacurrar

**[pacurrar.es](https://pacurrar.es)** · Web · 🚧 Coming soon · 🔒 Private code

> *Pa' currar*: to go to work.

## What it does

- **Every job offer in one place:** public-sector and private jobs in Aragón, plus remote ones, with Public / Company / Mixed modes.
- **One page per offer and per organisation,** plus guides for job seekers.
- **Alerts** *(planned)*: RSS, calendar, Telegram and push notifications.

## How it's built

- **Engine:** grows out of my own job scraper, which has been collecting offers for a while. It gathers offers from many sources and filters them with rules.
- **Backend:** Python, with server-rendered pages for SEO and a generated sitemap.
- **Ops:** Docker on my own server, with automated tests.
